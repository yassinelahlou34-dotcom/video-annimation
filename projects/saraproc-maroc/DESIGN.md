# SARAPROC MAROC — film de présentation

## Overview
Premium, spacious French presentation for an international hygiene meeting.
The website uses blue, white and industrial/sector photography; the supplied logos
introduce a royal blue and metallic silver. Adapt this identity to clean cinematic
layouts, without copying the website's navigation or marketing placeholders.
Alternate bright product-family panels with deep navy chapter moments.

## Colors
- `#2C418F`: royal blue sampled from the supplied laboratory logo; brand emphasis.
- `#26547F`: blue explicitly used in the website CSS; supporting accents.
- `#7D90B3`: silver-blue explicitly used in the website CSS; quiet ornament.
- `#0B1934`: navy canvas chosen to implement the user's navy direction.
- `#FFFFFF`: light canvas and logo panels; preserve original logo backgrounds.
- `#F3F6FA`: soft silver-white surface for editorial panels.
- `#182A45`: strong text on light backgrounds.
- `#53647B`: secondary text on light backgrounds.
- `#DCE5F2`: secondary text on navy, with strong contrast.

## Typography
Manrope variable, locally packaged: 300 for large editorial accents, 800 for titles,
500 for body labels. One family provides a restrained presentation register.
Titles 78–100px; key numbers 180–230px; body 30–34px; labels 24px minimum.
French accents preserved. Keep each sentence short and leave reading time.
The website's Inter UI typeface is replaced for this film's requested presentation
direction; logo typography remains the supplied artwork, never reconstructed.

## Elevation
Use broad white space, a thin silver rule, soft shadows around photo panels and
subtle radial illumination on navy. Photos occupy roughly half the frame and drift
slowly within their frames. Do not apply treatments or filters to any logos.

## Components
Editorial split: large French headline and short labels opposite sector imagery.
Family index: generously spaced two- or three-column labels, not a dense table.
Laboratory bridge: unmodified PDF page 2 centered on a white card for two seconds.
Medical identity: unmodified PDF page 1, with its original aspect ratio.
Official finale: actual white company logo from the website footer on navy.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Use verified website facts; correct spelling without enlarging the claims.
- Preserve logos exactly: no cropping, recoloring, tracing, warping or glow filters.
- Use uniform scale and translation only for logo animation.
- Use smooth opacity crossfades and refined entrances; no hard cuts or jumpy motion.
- No invented certification badges, statistics, product packaging or testimonials.
- No voiceover, no random sound effects, no busy backgrounds behind text.
- No text smaller than 24px or more than six short families in a single reading beat.
