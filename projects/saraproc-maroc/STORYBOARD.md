instruments, industry, surfaces, laundry, boiler, osmosis, pool, food-factory,
livestock-building, kitchen, cosmetics, base, soap, animals and agriculture images.

weaken the premium visual. Logo assets remain separate and unmodified.
Original files: assets/logos-original.pdf and BRIEF.txt.

## Source decisions
Use 2002/Fès from /a-propos. Do not invent a year-by-year history, certificate
scope or performance statistics. Prefer the website's exact family names over
unverified examples in the brief (e.g. an oven/soaking subfamily needs source
support). Certifications are site-listed names, not an independent certification
audit. The generic website description calls colorants a family; use that verified
term rather than asserting a specific pigment product.
